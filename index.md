# rclone-backup

rclone-backup is a private, single-user OAuth client. Its owner uses it with the open-source tool rclone (https://rclone.org/) to back up their own files from their own computer to their own Google Drive.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up and offers no accounts to anyone else.

Privacy Policy: https://starjw2010-cloud.github.io/rclone-backup/privacy.html
